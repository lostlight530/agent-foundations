## S37 — Google ADK conversational context

- Type: `E1_PRIMARY_STANDARD`
- URL: https://adk.dev/sessions/
- Use: explicit distinction between Session as the current conversation thread, session State as current-conversation data, and searchable Memory that can span sessions; lifecycle managed by SessionService and MemoryService.

## Combined architectural use

S33–S37 jointly support two bounded architecture statements:

1. protocol/task/session/memory scopes must be named rather than collapsed into one generic `state` concept
2. trace/trajectory evidence and final outcome evidence are complementary and must not be treated as interchangeable

Both remain `REFERENCE_ONLY` until an executable repository artifact and validation contract exist.
