# Historical Independent Audit Archive

This directory archives historical External Independent GPT audit, correction, evidence-accounting, reconciliation, and maintenance records for Agent Foundations. Archived bodies are moved with their original Git blobs; archive placement does not rewrite their historical observations or verification state.

## Scope boundaries

Not archived here:
- Jules Daily Research, Weekly Cascade, or Monthly Strategic Blueprint task artifacts
- active FOUNDATION contracts, source registry, evidence policy, specs, plans, schemas, checkers, or workflows
- current external-maintenance procedure/templates

## Archive classes

1. `01-corrections-and-errata/` — source/provenance errata and dated corrections to prior interpretation
2. `02-document-audits/` — independent audits of canonical/public document corpora
3. `03-stage-and-period-audits/` — provisional/as-of period reconciliation
4. `04-evidence-and-closure-ledgers/` — evidence accounting and natural-period closure ledgers
5. `05-maintenance-and-reconciliation/` — cross-period Jules cadence/content reconciliation and successor maintenance records
