# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they preserve the repository's evidence and provenance boundaries.

Before proposing a change, read `README.md` and the relevant contracts under `FOUNDATION/`, especially `FOUNDATION/EVIDENCE.md`, `FOUNDATION/PROVENANCE.md`, and `FOUNDATION/MAINTENANCE.md`.

## Contribution rules

- Keep claims bounded by named evidence, assumptions, implementation status, and validation status.
- Do not promote summaries, analogies, pseudocode, or external results into repository capability without executable repository evidence.
- Preserve stable Claim IDs and bilingual alignment where a change affects the verified core.
- Do not rewrite historical records to make later state appear earlier.
- Keep generated research streams separate from the independently maintained `FOUNDATION/` core.
- Include reproducible validation commands and results for executable or schema changes.

The maintainer retains final review and merge authority.
